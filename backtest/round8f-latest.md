## What it means
- **EPS ≥ 25% does not separate good breakouts from bad ones.** Trades that pass and trades that fail earn the same R, in both periods and under both stops. No growth bucket stands out (0–25% growth did as well as 50–100%). Sales growth and operating income are no better, so the "check operating EPS" idea (Berkshire case) has no support either: trades where official EPS fails but operating income passes earn the same as the rest. The rule shows **no harm**: the portfolio with the block was a little ahead in all four comparisons (+0.8 to +2.6 pts/yr), which the trade-level test cannot confirm.
- **The 14-day earnings block shows no effect.** Buying 0–14 days before a report earned the same R as buying further away; 7- and 21-day windows the same. The gap risk is real, but it is already paid for inside R, and the winners balance it.
- **1.4× volume and 5% extension stay.** No other cell beats the champion by the pre-set bar in both periods. More volume means fewer trades with about the same R per trade; 2.0× looked better in development under the live stop (CAGR 4.4 vs 2.5) but not in validation, and not under the original stop. By bucket: breakouts on 3×+ volume were the best group in three of four tables; entries 3–8% above the pivot were weaker than 0–3% in most — both small and uneven.
- **The 24-hour rule costs money.** Only 55–61% of BUY signals still rate BUY the next day, and the skipped ones were profitable (+0.11R / +0.02R live stop; +0.86R / +0.57R original stop). Waiting does not pick better trades (same R) and the portfolio earns less in all four comparisons (−0.7 to −3.2 pts/yr). With the original stop (the next-version candidate) it meets the pre-set HURTS bar.
- **Earnings gaps are not a setup under the live stop** (mean R about zero, 23% winners). With the **original stop** the picture flips: +1.2R per trade in both periods, intervals above zero, 15% winners — the same "few big winners" shape as breakouts. The combined portfolio was mixed (−0.6 / +2.5 pts/yr), so it is not added; it is a hypothesis to re-test once the stop decision is made.
