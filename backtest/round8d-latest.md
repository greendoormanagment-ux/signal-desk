# Exit ACTION rules — do "sell half" and "raise the stop" on warnings / before earnings help? (2026-11-11)

Pre-registered in `backtest/action-rules-preregistration.md` (locked on GitHub before the run). Code: `scripts/backtest/run_round8d.py`. The same breakout trades (all US, live gate) are re-run with each rule; "hold full" reproduces the engine exactly (40,846 of 40,846 trades). Earnings dates: SEC 8-K Item 2.02 filings for 2,340 stocks (stocks still listed today; most delisted companies have no SEC ID match), covering 48–69% of trades.

## What it means
- **All three rules reduce returns, in both periods, under both stops, with high confidence.** They cut the big winners: warning signs and earnings fear show up on the way *up* too, and selling into them gives away the trades that carry the system.
- With the **original stop** (where Round 8 points next) the damage is large: raising stops on warnings keeps only ~10% of the top winners' profit.
- This matches Round 4 (warning signs as full exits "cut winners more than they saved") and Round 8's main finding (the top 5% of trades make all the profit).
- **Portfolio view (approximate, one ordering, a partial sale booked at the final exit):** mixed — with the A2 stop, every rule showed a lower portfolio return in development but a higher one in validation (earlier exits free slots sooner); with the original stop every rule lowered the validation return. Not consistent; the per-trade result decides, as pre-registered.

**Next version:** remove SELL HALF on warnings, the warning stop-raise and SELL HALF BEFORE EARNINGS as instructions. Warning signs and the earnings date stay on screen as information only. **Live test:** v2.16 stays frozen through January 29 (agreed protocol); under the live A2 stop the measured cost is small (about −0.02 to −0.08R per trade).

**Limits:** earnings dates only for still-listed stocks (8-K/A amendments counted as report dates, so a few reports appear twice); SCALE_OUT (+20%, optional) not tested; market gate from the rules-based reading; portfolio figures approximate.
