# Round 5 — can Signal Desk beat SPY? (2026-10-22)

**Data:** free (Yahoo, survivorship-limited) · 657 S&P 500 members (point in time) · cost 0.07% per side + 0.03% per SPY switch · signals at the close, filled next open.
**Periods:** TUNE 2017-10-15 → 2021-12-31 · JUDGE (unseen) 2022-01-01 → 2025-12-31 · 2026 shown alone.

**What's new:** *idle SPY* = money not in a stock stays invested in SPY instead of cash (core + satellite in one account). *Combined* = Round 1's breakout + earnings-gap + pullback system with every exit on the 50-day line.

**Pass bar (set before the run):** beat SPY in BOTH tune and judge (median of 10 random-order runs), ≥ 70% of judge-period bootstrap resamples above zero, and max drawdown no worse than SPY's + 5 points.

> ⚠ **Free data:** companies that failed or were bought out are missing, so stock-picking results are flattered (SPY is not). Treat a pass here as 'worth the paid test', not as proof.
