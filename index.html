    HOLDINGS HEALTH CHECK · WEINSTEIN STAGE
    One holding per line: TICKER, shares, account (account optional — e.g. 401k, dad, kids; any account with 401k/IRA in its name is treated as tax-free to sell). The same stock can appear on several lines, one per account. After you cut a position, add trimmed to its line (e.g. TSLA, 323, trimmed) and you'll get a BUY BACK alert when it returns to Stage 2. Uses weekly bars and the 30-week average. Run it weekly or quarterly — it's for long-term holdings, not entries.
    HOW TO READ IT
    STAGE 2 · BUY BACK — was Stage 4 (or marked trimmed) and is back above a rising 30-week average. Buy back the half you sold.
    STAGE 2 · HEALTHY — price above a rising 30-week average. Hold; it's okay to add.
    STAGE 2 · PULLBACK — dipped below a still-rising average. Normal; watch that the average keeps rising.
    STAGE 3 · WARNING — the average has gone flat after an uptrend (topping). Stop adding; decide your exit level now.
    STAGE 4 · EARLY — below a falling average, but for fewer than 4 weeks. Get ready to act.
    STAGE 4 · PIVOT — below a falling average for 4+ weeks. Your rule: cut half or more, no debate.
    STAGE 1 · BASING — flat after a decline. Not a buy until it breaks into Stage 2.
